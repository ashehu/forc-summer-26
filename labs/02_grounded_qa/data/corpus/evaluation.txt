Northbridge Peer Methods Clinic — evaluation note

The evaluation compared 32 clinic participants with 30 students on a wait-list. By week eight, 69 percent of clinic participants had submitted a preregistration or analysis plan, compared with 43 percent of the wait-list group. The largest observed difference was therefore in submission of a preregistration or analysis plan. Self-rated research confidence rose slightly in both groups, with no clear difference between them. Because participation was voluntary, the comparison does not establish that the clinic caused the observed difference.
