TEACHING EXCERPT
The cover labels the document "FOR SALES TRAINING PURPOSES ONLY" and "DO NOT DISSEMINATE." It introduces a sales aid for once-daily extended-release hydromorphone.

The guide tells sales representatives to use a countdown-clock image to make the message memorable. It frames patients with chronic pain as "clock-watchers" who organize ordinary activities around the next dose. The instructions explain how the visual device can begin a sales conversation and reinforce the product's once-daily dosing story.

This document is evidence of a sales-message implementation strategy. It is not evidence that the message was clinically appropriate, that prescribers accepted it, or that patients experienced the described benefit.
