Interviewer: What made the peer-methods clinic useful or difficult to use?

Participant: The best part was arriving with one concrete decision. When I brought a whole chapter, the conversation stayed vague. When I brought one model choice, we actually resolved it.

Interviewer: What got in the way of attending?

Participant: Tuesday evenings conflicted with our student review, so I missed two sessions. Moving it earlier would help, but a remote option would probably help more.

Interviewer: Did anything change in your research process?

Participant: I started writing down the decision I needed before asking for help. That saved time with my adviser too. I still wanted a specialist for the final model, because the peer group could not tell whether one assumption was defensible.

Interviewer: What should the program do next?

Participant: Keep the small groups, offer a remote session, and publish the facilitator topics in advance. I would make the clinic mandatory.
