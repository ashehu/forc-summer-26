TEACHING EXCERPT
The document says the Walgreens plan included three aims: save lives, deter misuse and addiction, and prevent future misuse of prescription drugs. It reports that Walgreens was stocking Narcan in more than 8,000 pharmacies and expanding access without a prescription where legally permitted.

The statement also reports that Walgreens had installed 600 medication-disposal kiosks across 45 states and the District of Columbia. It says those kiosks collected 155 tons of unwanted medications during the first 18 months. The company described plans to expand to 1,500 kiosks and reported educational efforts aimed at patients, teenagers, pharmacists, and communities.

These are actions and results reported in a corporate statement. This excerpt does not independently verify implementation, coverage, or effects on overdose deaths.
